FIXZEERS V2 FRONTEND
====================
Open index.html to start.

This is a connected multi-page frontend prototype. It includes:
- Home / landing page
- Search/results
- Customer login/register/recovery
- Professional registration/login/dashboard/jobs
- Professional profile and reputation
- Booking, job status and review flow
- About, How It Works, Contact, Help, Careers
- Draft Privacy, Terms, Refunds and Community Guidelines

The forms and job data are demo-only. No backend, database, authentication,
payments, OTP delivery, real booking, or production legal system is connected yet.
Government-ID verification is intentionally not required in the initial onboarding.
