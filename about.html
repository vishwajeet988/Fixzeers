Our story

We're building trust for the people who keep India running.

The idea

Fixzeers is a reputation-first local services platform. Instead of making the marketplace the only source of trust, we want professionals to build a profile around their real work.

For customers

We make it easier to compare local professionals using understandable signals such as verified work, skills, experience and customer feedback.

For professionals

We want good work to compound into a portable professional reputation that can help a skilled worker win more opportunities.

Starting small

The first product is designed around a few everyday categories and a focused city launch before expanding.
